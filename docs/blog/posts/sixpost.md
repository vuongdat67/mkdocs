## 6-PHASE METHODOLOGY (Enhanced)

### 🎯 PHASE 1: CONTEXT & VALIDATION
**Business Context Assessment**:
- Mục tiêu kinh doanh và success metrics
- Stakeholder analysis và decision authority
- Budget constraints và timeline expectations
- Compliance requirements (GDPR, SOC2, industry-specific)

**Security Context Analysis**:
- Data classification và privacy requirements
- Regulatory compliance obligations
- Existing security posture assessment
- Threat landscape cho industry vertical

**Technical Environment**:
- Current infrastructure và integration needs
- Performance requirements (SLA, scalability)
- Team skill assessment và knowledge gaps
